Welcome! I am **Keyu Cui**, a Ph.D. candidate in Information Systems at Xi’an Jiaotong University. From 2024 to 2025, I was a visiting Ph.D. student at HEC Paris, jointly supervised by [Xitong Li](https://www.hec.edu/en/faculty-research/faculty-directory/faculty-member/li-xitong) and [Shan Liu](http://gr.xjtu.edu.cn/web/shanliu). I received my bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Finance from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China in 2020.

My research lies at the intersection of digital platforms, artificial intelligence, and computational social science, with a particular focus on how emerging digital technologies reshape platform governance, user behavior, and knowledge production. My work examines digital platforms, organizational and individual adoption of information technologies and AI, user-generated content, and digital content pricing. More recently, I have expanded this research agenda to AI for Science and Social Science, exploring how AI transforms scientific innovation, collaboration, and the production of knowledge. 

Across these projects, I primarily combine large-scale observational data with causal inference, machine learning, and computational approaches, while increasingly incorporating AI agent-based experiments to study emerging forms of human–AI interaction.

My work has received revision opportunities at **MIS Quarterly**, **Nature Communications**, and **Information Systems Research**.

Feel free to contact me at [cuikeyu123@outlook.com](mailto:cuikeyu123@outlook.com).
